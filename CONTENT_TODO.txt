CONTENT TO FILL IN BEFORE LAUNCH
=================================

All content lives in src/data/content.ts.

1) siteConfig
   - upworkUrl        your Upwork profile link (used by every "Hire" button)
   - upworkRating     your real Upwork rating
   - email, githubUrl, linkedinUrl

2) team
   - github / linkedin   personal links for each member
   - photoUrl            add a photo path (for example "/team/nima.jpg" in public/).
                         Without it, the initials avatar with the glitch effect is shown.

3) projects
   Four placeholder projects. Replace title, description, tech and kind.

4) stats
   24 projects, 18 clients, 6 years are placeholder numbers. Use real ones.

5) testimonials
   Three placeholders. Paste real Upwork reviews and client names.

6) Badge text "[Top Rated badge]" in src/components/Trust.tsx
   Replace it with your real Upwork badge or remove it.
